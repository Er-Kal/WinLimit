# Privacy Policy for WinLimit

WinLimit is committed to your privacy. Because this tool monitors active processes to enforce your focus schedules, we want to be 100% transparent about your data.

### 1. Data Collection
We collect only the information necessary to provide cross-device synchronization:
- **Account Info:** Email/Username (for authentication).
- **Configurations:** Your blocklists, schedules, and app names you have chosen to limit.

### 2. Data We DO NOT Collect
- **No Activity Logs:** We do not track what you are doing inside apps.
- **No Keylogging:** We do not monitor keystrokes or screen content.
- **No Local Data Access:** We only see the *names* of the processes you have explicitly asked us to monitor.

### 3. Data Usage
Data is used exclusively to sync your settings. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.

### 4. Technical Enforcement
WinLimit uses `Process.Kill()` locally on your machine. This action is triggered solely by the rules you define. No remote commands from our servers can terminate processes on your computer.
